# Roadmap

Order may change; no dates are promised.

## Now

- Challenges and a free-track battlepass, earned by play only.
- Loot tuning for solo and duo play.
- A real-client QA bot that plays the game the way a person does.

## Next

- Separate solo and duo leaderboards.
- Group-size cap and partner-only base and lock access.
- Ravens, repair box, backpack and in-game inventory.
- Scrap and dressing room.
- Legal pages finished and approved.
- Installer packaging for the launcher.

## Before launch

- Full solo and duo rules, including anti-teaming, published.

## Later

- A staging server and a small private beta.
- Publishing source for the parts we choose, under GPL-3.0.
- Further content once client and server work can be built together.

Clans and bunkers are deliberately left out. H1Z1EU is built for solo and duo play, with groups of two at most.
