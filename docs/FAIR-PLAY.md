# Fair play and coins

- **Groups of two at most.** Three or more players working together breaks the rules. The full rules will be published before launch.
- **Coins are earned by playing.** You earn them for time spent in game and for completing things there.
- **Coins can't be bought.** Nothing can be bought with money.
- **Coins and items can't be sold, swapped or withdrawn.** They stay on your account.
- **Crate odds are published.** Every crate shows the chance of each reward, with a version number. A crate can't go on sale in the store until its odds are public, and every roll is logged and can be replayed.
- **Bans can be appealed.** Staff actions are recorded in an audit log.
- **Your data is yours.** You can export it and delete your account from the website.
